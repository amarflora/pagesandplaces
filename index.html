Pages & Places

A handwritten travel journal for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad. By Infinity Tech.

Write by hand or type, pin your photos, see every place you've written on a map, and collect a passport stamp in every new city. Private, in your own iCloud.

Getting help

In the app, open ⋯ → Help & tips for a guide to every feature, or replay the welcome tour.

Still stuck? Email placespages15@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Subscription

Pages & Places is free for 7 days, then a yearly subscription. Cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Without a subscription your diary stays readable. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the app's Settings → Restore purchases.

Your diary on a new phone

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, install the app, and your diary appears.
