Privacy Policy

Pages & Places, by Infinity Tech · Last updated 8 November 2026

Pages & Places is a private journal. We don't collect any of your data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Your diary

Everything you write, draw, photograph, pin or remind yourself about is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database). Only you can read it. We can't see it, and we never receive a copy.

Location

If you add a place to a day, its coordinates and name are saved with that day in your diary. Place names and countries are looked up with Apple Maps on your device. Your location is only used when you ask for it (or turn on "Add location automatically"), and it is never sent to us.

Photos and camera

Photos you choose or take are copied onto your diary pages. The app only sees the photos you pick. If you ask, it can save a copy to your photo library.

Journaling Suggestions

On iPhone and iPad, you can start a page from Apple's Journaling Suggestions. The app only receives the one moment you choose, and it's saved in your diary like anything else you write.

Reminders, alarms and Face ID

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app is only told whether unlocking succeeded.

Subscription

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We don't receive your payment details. Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete pages or days in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete Pages & Places data.
