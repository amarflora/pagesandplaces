Privacy Policy

Bodhi Path

Bodhi Path, by Infinity Tech · Last updated 10 November 2026

Bodhi Path is a journal for grown-ups and a story book for children. We don't collect any of your data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The app works without an internet connection.

Your journal

Everything you write, draw or keep in the journal (your writing, handwriting, moods, three good things, kind acts and photos) is stored only on your device. We can't see it, and we never receive a copy. If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or your computer, your journal is included in that backup, which belongs to you.

Children

The stories, quizzes and colouring pages collect nothing. Colouring pages and progress (stories read, lotuses grown) are saved on the device only. The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Photos

If you add a photo to a day, the app only sees the photo you pick, and keeps a copy in your journal on the device.

Face ID, Touch ID and reminders

If you lock your journal, Face ID or Touch ID is handled by iOS; the app is only told whether unlocking succeeded. The daily reminder is scheduled on your device.

Sharing

When you share a teaching or a verse picture, it goes only where you choose to send it, using the iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it has stored.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
