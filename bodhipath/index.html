Bodhi Path

A Buddhist journal, and stories of the Buddha for children

What it is

For grown-ups, a teaching of the Buddha for each day, in his own words and in plain words, with a journal and a gentle question to write about. For children, 30 illustrated stories (the life of the Buddha in 20 chapters, and Jātaka tales) that are read aloud, react when touched, and come with a "What would you do?" quiz and a colouring page.

Questions

Where is my journal kept? Only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. It is included in your usual iCloud or computer backup of the device.

Can I lock it? Yes. In Settings, turn on "Lock my journal with Face ID" (or Touch ID). The stories and the day's teaching are never locked, so children can use them.

The stories aren't reading aloud. Check that "Read stories aloud" is on in Settings and the volume is up. The page can also be read without sound.

How do I see the Pali? Tap any line of the Buddha's words, or turn on "Always show the Pali" in Settings.

The daily reminder doesn't come. Turn on notifications for Bodhi Path in the Settings app, then turn on the reminder in Bodhi Path's Settings.

Sources

The Buddha's words are translated by Bhikkhu Sujato, with Pali from the Mahāsaṅgīti edition, both published by SuttaCentral and dedicated to the public domain. The retellings of the stories and the plain-language teachings are written for this app.
